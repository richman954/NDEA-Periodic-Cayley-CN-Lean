# Experiment 003: Euclidean Resolvent Contraction

Prove, for every finite dimension `n`, complex Hermitian matrix `A`, and real
`alpha`, that the continuous linear operator induced by
`(I + i alpha A)⁻¹` on `EuclideanSpace Complex (Fin n)` has operator norm at most
one. Establish this through the inherited Experiment 002 Cayley identities and norm
preservation, not through an independent spectral argument.

Only Step 1 is authorized. See `FROZEN_SPECIFICATION.md` for the exact target,
controls, assurance gate, and exclusions.
