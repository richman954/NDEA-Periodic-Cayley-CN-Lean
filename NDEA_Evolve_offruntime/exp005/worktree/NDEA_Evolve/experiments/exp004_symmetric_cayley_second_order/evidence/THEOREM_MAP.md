# Experiment 004 source-to-theorem map

All production declarations below are in namespace `NDEAEvolve.Exp004`.
This map identifies the intended endpoints; final verification status is
established by the separately selected build receipts and assurance audit.

Source: `NDEAEvolve/Experiments/Exp004/SymmetricCayleyLocal.lean`.

- `symmetricStepHat`: the ordered quarter/half/quarter Cayley composition.
- `symmetric_cayley_exp_local_opNorm_le`: explicit cubic local comparison

Source: `NDEAEvolve/Experiments/Exp004/SymmetricCayleyGlobal.lean`.

## Attributable controls

Source: `experiments/exp004_symmetric_cayley_second_order/controls/lean/NegativeControls.lean`.
Namespace: `NDEAEvolve.Exp004.Controls`.

- `zero_time_exact`, `zero_power_identity`, `zero_generators_exact`,
  `zero_dimension_exact`, and `negative_time_bound` instantiate edge cases.
- `scalarCayley_matrix_entry`, `scalarSymmetric_matrix_entry`,
  `symmetricStepHat_matrix_bridge`, and `scalarSymmetric_operator_bridge`
  connect the exact scalar calculations and matrix products to the production
  continuous-linear-map scheme.
- `symmetric_vs_nonsymmetric_detected` and `wrong_quarterstep_detected`
  use exact rational real parts at h=2. These are method-identification
  controls, not rate lower bounds; scalar generators commute.
- `finite_step_exponential_equality_rejected` distinguishes the actual
  symmetric matrix step from the exact scalar exponential at A=0,B=I,t=π.
  It rejects finite-step equality, not the stated small-step upper bound.
- `pauliX_hermitian`, `pauliZ_hermitian`, `pauli_noncommutation`, and
  `nonsymmetric_quadratic_mismatch` give a noncommuting Hermitian witness
  for the quadratic mixed-term obstruction in nonsymmetric splitting.
